Hello, my name is Giao Phan.

You might notice my portfolio website has a rather curious name: untitled_being.txt. Every creative journey begins the same way. Before a single idea takes shape, everything starts as untitled, a blank page waiting for a story. I've always loved the .txt file format. It is one of the humblest things in the digital world, yet quietly powerful. Change its extension, and it can become almost anything.
Pair that with _being, a simple presence, a human life, and you find me. Like everyone else, I hold the freedom to begin again and become whoever I choose to be. Last year, I created a detailed MULTI-DESIGN PORTFOLIO on Behance and genuinely enjoyed every moment of making it. To keep that creative spark alive, I built this website, simple, rooted in my own aesthetic, and continuing the story I started.

Welcome to my Multidisciplinary portfolio!
